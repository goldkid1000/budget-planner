    My money

    This app doesn't log into your bank. No bank login, no passwords, nothing that could expose your account. Instead, bring in a bank statement — the app reads it right here on your device.

    How it works: in your bank's app or website, download your statement as a CSV file (or copy its text). Drop it here — the app reads it on this device and fills in your balance. Your statement never leaves this device.
