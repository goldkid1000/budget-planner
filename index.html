    🗓️ My schedule

    Pick a tab — Classes, Practices or Workouts — then add each one with the days it happens and its start and end times. Add as many as you need: tap Add, then “add another” to keep going on the same tab. Times are exact to the minute: 8:00 to 8:50 is 50 minutes (0.83 hrs), never rounded. The week view below lines everything up against your planned work shifts from the school-week planner and flags any overlap, and ✨ Suggest my work hours routes around all three — so add practices and workouts here with their times (a tag up in the planner has no times to work around).

    ✨ Suggest my work hours

    Pick a weekly target and I'll fit work shifts around your classes, practices and workouts — 30 minutes of breathing room before and after each one, Sundays and meet days off, shifts of 2–6 hours between 7:00 AM and 10:00 PM. You'll see the whole week before anything changes.

    Busy-day guard: if a day's classes, practice + workouts add up to 5+ hrs, Suggest won't put work on it — that day stays yours.
